Thoughtful Memorial Options

🔥

Private Cremation

Your pet is cremated individually with guaranteed return of ashes. Our most selected option for families seeking certainty and peace of mind.

🌿

Aquamation

Water-based cremation that's gentle on the environment. Uses 90% less energy than flame cremation with the same dignified results.

🕊️

Communal Memorial

A respectful, affordable option where ashes are scattered in our memorial gardens. Includes a commemorative plaque.

💎

Memorial Jewelry

Keep your companion close with custom pendants, rings, and bracelets designed to hold a small portion of ashes securely.

🌳

Tree Memorial

Biodegradable urns that transform into living trees. Choose from oak, maple, or dogwood to create a lasting living tribute.
