How It Works

A Gentle Process

We've designed our services to be as simple and stress-free as possible during this emotional time.

1

Contact Us

Call us anytime, day or night. We'll guide you through the options and arrange pickup or drop-off.

2

Choose Services

Select the cremation type, urn, and any memorial keepsakes that feel right for your family.

3

Gentle Care

Your pet is treated with dignity throughout. Optional witnessed cremation available.

4

Return Home

Ashes are returned in 3-5 days in your chosen urn, or scattered in our memorial garden.
