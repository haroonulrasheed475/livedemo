About Elena

Where Art Meets Purpose

With over fifteen years in the entertainment industry, I've learned that every role—whether on camera or in life—requires authenticity, vulnerability, and courage. My journey began in theater, evolved into film and television, and expanded into writing and directing my own narratives.

Parallel to my creative career, I pursued nutrition science and became a certified dietitian, recognizing the profound connection between physical health and creative expression. Today, I blend these worlds as a mindset coach, helping fellow artists and professionals break through limiting beliefs and step into their power.
