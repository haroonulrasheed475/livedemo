Welcome

An intimate overnight experience designed for those who seek stillness. Located within the ancient walls of York, No. 12 offers a sanctuary of calm, curated detail, and timeless comfort.

The Experience

The Room

Every element has been considered. From the weight of the linen to the scent in the air, the room is designed to feel like a private residence—quiet, warm, and entirely yours for the night.

The Morning

Morning arrives softly. A bespoke breakfast is left at your door, featuring local Yorkshire produce and freshly brewed coffee, allowing you to rise at your own pace.

The City

Step outside into the cobbled streets of York. We provide a hand-selected guide to the city's hidden corners—bookshops, quiet gardens, and historic passages away from the crowds.

Our Story

A Personal Vision

No. 12 was born from a desire to offer something different. After years of traveling and staying in places that felt either too impersonal or overly performative, we wanted to create a space that simply feels right.


We believe luxury is not about excess, but about care. The care taken in selecting a pillow, the care in choosing a painting for the wall, or the care in writing a welcome note by hand.


York is a city of stories. We invite you to write your own here, in a space designed for rest, reflection, and gentle discovery.
